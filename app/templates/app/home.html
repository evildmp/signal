Using Signal

Signal allows you to record and share how you feel, using a grid: from feeling bad to feeling good on one axis, and from settled to aroused on the other.

Place a dot by selecting a position on the grid. Your own dots appear in orange. Dots fade as they age and disappear after two weeks.

Anonymity

Signal guarantees anonymity, unless you choose to share your identity. Unless you choose to publish your ownership of a dot, only your browser knows which dots belong to you.

Flagging a dot

If you are concerned by the text published alongside a dot, flag it for review.
